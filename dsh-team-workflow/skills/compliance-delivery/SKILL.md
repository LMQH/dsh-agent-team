# Compliance Delivery

Review the actual task, revision, authorization record, protected paths, effective ADRs, security controls, and sensitive-data exposure. Classify each conclusion as pass, risk, block, or needs-authorization. Every non-pass conclusion needs a concrete path/construct, impact, and remediation. Never grant the authorization you are reviewing.
